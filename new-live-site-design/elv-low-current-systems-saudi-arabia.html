Datacore is a Saudi ELV (extra-low-voltage) and low-current systems contractor. We design, supply, install, commission and maintain the low-current backbone of a building — the systems that run on extra-low voltage rather than mains power — as a single integrator with one accountable handover. We have been integrating across the Kingdom, the UAE and India since 2007.

What ELV / low-current covers

Our ELV scope runs across nine disciplines and 38 services: IT network infrastructure and structured cabling, data centre design and implementation, surveillance and security (CCTV, access control), audio-visual and meeting rooms, digital signage and video walls, public address and fire alarm, IPTV, and the professional services that tie them together. One contractor owning all of it is what turns a set of systems into an intelligent building.

Standards and Saudi compliance

Every discipline is designed to its relevant standard — structured cabling to TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801, public address and fire alarm to EN 54 — and life-safety systems are engineered to the Saudi Building Code (SBC 801) and to Civil Defense approval requirements. We justify each design against the standard and your operational requirement, then commission with documented test results, so the system passes inspection and performs in service.

Built for Vision 2030 projects

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 giga-projects and smart-city programmes are raising demand for well-engineered, integrated low-current systems. We start on site with a measured survey — not a pick from a catalogue — produce a design justified against the requirement, install and commission with our own engineers, and back it with response-time maintenance after handover.

Frequently asked

What does ELV / low current include?

Extra-low-voltage systems: data networks, structured cabling, audio-visual, CCTV, access control, public address and fire alarm, IPTV and related building systems — everything that runs on low current rather than mains power.

Are you a licensed low-current contractor in Saudi Arabia?

Datacore delivers low-current / ELV systems across the Kingdom from our Riyadh headquarters. We design life-safety systems to SBC 801 and Civil Defense requirements and commission with documented test results.

Do you take on the whole ELV scope or single systems?

Both — but delivering the full ELV scope as one integrator with a single handover is our core strength and where we remove the most risk for a project.

Do you provide maintenance after handover?

Yes — Annual Maintenance Contracts combine preventive maintenance, proactive monitoring and response-time-backed technical support, with full-time on-site staffing available.
