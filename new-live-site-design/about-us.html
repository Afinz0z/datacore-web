ABOUT US

Leading IT & AV integrators delivering seamless, secure, and future-ready technology solutions.

At Datacore, we deliver trusted IT and AV systems with certified experts, strong partnerships, nationwide execution and advanced integration capabilities

OUR STORY

Founded in 2007, Datacore Solutions began with a vision to deliver reliable, future-ready technology services. In 2010, we expanded to Riyadh, strengthening our presence in Saudi Arabia and positioning ourselves at the heart of the Kingdom’s growing digital landscape. Over the years, we have mastered the design and delivery of comprehensive network solutions, systems integration, cabling infrastructure, point-to-point wireless networks, audio-visual solutions, LED displays, professional audio, public address, and fire alarm systems.


With more than 100 skilled professionals and a portfolio of 1,000+ satisfied customers, Datacore Solutions has become a trusted partner for IT and AV projects across the country. Our team carries top industry certifications and collaborates with leading global manufacturers to deliver high-quality, dependable solutions.

 

From designing intelligent environments to deploying enterprise-grade networks, implementing advanced security systems, and enabling seamless collaboration across teams, Datacore provides end-to-end solutions tailored for modern enterprises. Our focus on quality, reliability, and innovation allows us to deliver technology ecosystems that are scalable, efficient, and future-ready. With Datacore as your trusted integration partner, you gain the confidence to navigate digital transformation with clarity and excellence.

 

Our success is built on one core commitment—executing every project without fail. This unwavering dedication drives our growth and defines who we are.
