Datacore is a network solutions provider headquartered in Riyadh. We design, supply, install, test and maintain the IT network infrastructure a business runs on — the structured cabling, fibre, switching, wireless and data-centre layers — as one integrator, with documented test results at handover. We have delivered network infrastructure for education, government and enterprise clients across Saudi Arabia since 2007.

Network infrastructure we deliver

Our network scope covers structured cabling (copper and fibre), fibre-optic backbones between floors and buildings, enterprise switching and routing, wireless / Wi-Fi design and survey, IP telephony, UPS and power protection, and the data-centre infrastructure — racks, containment, cooling and cabling — that houses it. We design the passive layer for the life of the building and the active layer to grow into it.

Designed and tested to standard

Structured cabling is designed to TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801, installed to category and fibre counts with headroom, and — critically — tested and certified, so every link is proven rather than assumed. We start with a measured survey of the site and the operational requirement, not a catalogue pick, and hand over a labelled, documented, warrantable system.

Riyadh-based, Kingdom-wide

Our headquarters and engineering team are in Riyadh (Office 503, Dabbab Complex, Dabbab Street), which means local response for Riyadh projects and coordinated delivery across the Kingdom. As Vision 2030 developments push network density and 5G-ready passive infrastructure, we design backbones with the fibre and pathway headroom to carry what comes next.

Frequently asked

Are you based in Riyadh?

Yes — our headquarters and engineering team are in Riyadh (Office 503, Dabbab Complex, Dabbab Street), and we deliver network projects across Saudi Arabia.

Do you test and certify the cabling?

Yes. Every structured-cabling link is tested and certified to TIA-568 / ISO-IEC 11801, and we hand over documented test results — the passive layer is the part you cannot cheaply revisit, so we prove it.

Can you deliver the network and the AV / security together?

Yes — network, audio-visual and security as one integrator with a single handover is our core strength, which removes the seams between systems.

Do you support the network after installation?

Yes — Annual Maintenance Contracts provide preventive maintenance, proactive monitoring and response-time-backed support, with on-site staffing options.
