Datacore is an AV and network systems integrator in Saudi Arabia. We deliver a building's IT network, audio-visual, security and life-safety systems as one team, with a single point of accountability and one handover. Splitting those scopes across separate contractors is where projects fail at the seams; owning them together is where we take the risk out. We have integrated across the Kingdom since 2007.

AV and network, delivered together

The network and the AV are not separate projects — the meeting room depends on the switch, the video wall on the backbone, the microphone on the control system, the public address on the fire-alarm interface. We design and install both layers to their standards (TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801 for the network, EN 54 for life-safety audio), so they work as one system on day one.

A worked example

Our One World International School (OWIS) Riyadh campus shows the model end to end: network, Wi-Fi, access control, classroom AV and an auditorium, all delivered by one team. Prince Sattam bin Abdulaziz University, the Arab Open University auditorium and TAQEEM headquarters are further examples of AV, IT and security handed over as one accountable scope.

Nine disciplines, one integrator

Across nine disciplines and 38 services — network, data centre, surveillance and security, meeting rooms, audio-visual, digital signage, public address and fire alarm, IPTV and professional services — one integrator means one design intent, one cable schedule, one commissioning plan and one team to call afterwards. For Vision 2030 projects that expect smart, connected buildings, that is the difference between systems that coexist and systems that work together.

Frequently asked

What does a systems integrator do?

A systems integrator designs and delivers a building's separate technical systems — network, AV, security, life-safety — as one coordinated whole, so they work together rather than merely coexist, under a single point of accountability.

Why use one integrator instead of separate contractors?

It removes the integration risk that lives between systems: the network, AV, security and fire-alarm interfaces are designed and commissioned together, with one team accountable for the result and the handover.

What is your largest integrated project?

Our One World International School (OWIS) Riyadh campus is a full example — network, Wi-Fi, access control, classroom AV and an auditorium, all delivered by one team.

Where do you operate?

Headquartered in Riyadh, delivering across Saudi Arabia, with group offices in Dubai and Kozhikode supporting the wider region since 2007.
