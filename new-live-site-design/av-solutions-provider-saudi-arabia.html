Datacore is a Saudi audio-visual solutions provider that designs, supplies, installs, commissions and maintains professional AV systems as a single integrator. We have delivered AV environments for education, government and enterprise clients across the Kingdom since 2007 — from a single boardroom to a full auditorium — and hand the room over working, documented and supported.

The AV systems we deliver

Our audio-visual scope spans smart meeting rooms and boardrooms, auditoriums and multipurpose halls, LED video walls and fine-pitch displays, digital signage networks, command-and-control (SOC/NOC) rooms, professional audio and public-address, video conferencing and unified-communications integration, and BYOD/wireless presentation. Because we also deliver the network, security and life-safety layers, the AV integrates with the building instead of sitting beside it.

Why a single AV integrator

Rooms fail at the seams — between the display and the network, the microphone and the control system, the AV and the fire-alarm interface. Delivering AV, IT and ELV as one integrator with one handover removes those seams. Our One World International School (OWIS) Riyadh campus is a full example: network, Wi-Fi, access control, classroom AV and an auditorium, all by one team; the Arab Open University auditorium and TAQEEM headquarters are AV-led examples of the same discipline.

Designed to the standard, built for Saudi Arabia

Every AV design is justified against your operational requirement and the relevant standard, and life-safety audio (public address and voice evacuation) is designed to standards such as EN 54 and to Saudi Building Code (SBC 801) and Civil Defense requirements. As the Kingdom's Vision 2030 projects raise the bar for smart, connected spaces, we design for intelligibility, reliability and easy operation — then commission with documented test results.

Frequently asked

Do you cover the whole of Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We are headquartered in Riyadh and deliver AV projects across the Kingdom, with group offices in Dubai and Kozhikode supporting the wider region.

Can you handle AV, IT and security on one project?

Yes — delivering audio-visual, network and life-safety systems as one integrator with a single handover is our core strength, which removes the integration risk of splitting a room across several contractors.

Which AV brands do you work with?

We select equipment to fit the requirement rather than the other way around, and partner with leading manufacturers including Crestron, Extron, Q-SYS, Biamp, Shure, Samsung and LG, among others.

Do you maintain the system after installation?

Yes. We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) combining preventive maintenance, proactive monitoring and response-time-backed support, plus on-site staffing options.
