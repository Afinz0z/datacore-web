--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the Datacore company website. Datacore is a "low-current
systems integrator" — a company that designs and installs the
technology inside buildings: computer networks, audio-visual systems
(screens, sound, meeting rooms), data centres, security and CCTV,
public-address and fire-alarm systems, and more. The website presents
what Datacore does, shows example projects, lists products, and lets
visitors get in touch or request a price quote. It works in both
English and Arabic.

3. A TOUR OF THE PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Every page comes in two languages (English and Arabic). Here is what
each page is for:

  • Home ................. The front page. Introduces the company,
                          highlights the main services, shows featured
                          projects, client logos, and latest articles.

  • About Us ............ The company story, leadership, the team
                          (with photos of the Riyadh and Kozhikode
                          teams), values, and what makes Datacore
                          different.

  • Services ............ The full list of what Datacore offers,
                          organised into nine areas. Each individual
                          service also has its own detailed page
                          (there are 38 service pages in total).

  • Products ............ A searchable catalogue of hardware. Visitors
                          can filter by category, brand, or
                          availability, add items to a "request" list,
                          and send it to Datacore for a price quote.
                          (No payment happens on the site — it simply
                          creates a quote request.)

  • Projects ............ Real example projects ("case studies") with
                          photos, showing work Datacore has delivered.

  • Insights ............ Articles and news about the industry.

  • Contact ............. A contact form, the three office locations
                          with a map, and phone/email details.

  • Careers ............. Job information for people who want to work
                          at Datacore.

  • Terms, Privacy, and a "page not found" (404) page — the standard
    supporting pages every website needs.

4. WHAT VISITORS CAN DO ON THE SITE (FEATURES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Switch language — a button in the top bar flips the whole site
    between English and Arabic. Arabic reads right-to-left, and the
    layout flips to match.

  • Dark mode — a sun/moon button switches the site between a light
    and a dark colour scheme, for comfortable viewing.

  • Quick-contact chat bubble — a round button in the bottom-right
    corner (with the Datacore logo). Clicking it opens quick ways to
    get in touch: WhatsApp, phone call, email, or an enquiry form.
    (It is NOT an automated robot chat — it simply offers contact
    options.)

  • Request a quote — on the Products page, visitors build a list of
    items and send it as a quote request. A small cart button labelled
    "Your request" in the corner keeps count and gently animates each
    time an item is added.

  • Mobile-friendly — the site rearranges itself neatly on phones and
    tablets, and shows a handy "Call" and "Request a consultation" bar
    fixed at the bottom of the screen on small devices.

  • Gentle animations — numbers count up, cards lift slightly when you
    hover over them, and the top bar shrinks as you scroll. These are
    automatically switched off for visitors who have asked their
    device to reduce on-screen motion (an accessibility setting).

7. HOW THE WEBSITE IS PUT TOGETHER (SIMPLE VERSION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

You do not need this section to use the site, but it helps to know:

  • The site is made of ordinary web files (pages, styles, images).
    There is no complicated database or login system to maintain.

  • Most pages are generated automatically from a set of small
    "recipe" programs, so the whole site can be rebuilt consistently.

  • Making a change (for example, editing wording, swapping a photo,
    or adding a product) is done by editing these files and then
    "publishing" the updated files. This is a job for whoever maintains
    the site technically.

  • If you want a change made, the simplest path is to write down
    exactly what you want (which page, what should it say, which photo)
    and pass it to your web maintainer.

8. SEARCH ENGINES AND AI (WHY THE SITE SHOULD BE EASY TO FIND)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The site has been prepared so that Google and AI assistants (such as
ChatGPT and similar answer tools) can understand it well:

  • Every page carries hidden, structured "fact cards" that tell
    search engines who Datacore is, where its offices are, and what it
    does. This helps the company appear correctly in search results
    and AI answers.

  • English and Arabic pages are linked together so search engines show
    the right language to the right person.

  • Each page has a proper title and description, a shareable preview
    image (used when a page is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, etc.), and
    is listed in a "sitemap" (a directory of all pages for search
    engines).

  • All of this points at the official address www.datacore.com.sa, so
    the credit builds up on the company's real domain.

10. A SMALL GLOSSARY (PLAIN MEANINGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Web address / URL — the location you type to visit a page.
  • Publish / deploy — to put updated files onto the internet so
    visitors see the changes.
  • Staging / preview — a test copy of the site, not yet the final
    public home.
  • SEO — "Search Engine Optimisation": making a site easy for Google
    to understand and rank.
  • Structured data — hidden, machine-readable facts on a page that
    help search engines and AI understand it.
  • Responsive — a site that reshapes itself to fit phones, tablets,
    and computers.
  • Right-to-left (RTL) — the direction Arabic text reads and lays out.
