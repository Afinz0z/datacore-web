Comprehensive AV solution for TAQEEM HQ

A single, easy-to-use audio-visual standard applied across every space at the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's valuation regulator.

Background

TAQEEM, the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, regulates the valuation profession in the Kingdom. It partnered with Datacore to revamp the audio-visual infrastructure across its headquarters and turn it into a modern, tech-enabled workspace.

The challenge

One consistent AV experience had to work across very different spaces — a multipurpose room, meeting rooms, reception, a customer-care office, a control room, a thinkpod, executive offices and a theatre — while staying easy to use and ready to grow. Displays, controllers, digital-signage players and wireless presentation all had to be integrated and operate smoothly together.

The solution

Datacore designed a bespoke AV ecosystem on state-of-the-art equipment — Samsung and Epson displays, Shure audio, Bose, Barco, Televic conferencing, and AMX and Extron control and switching, with Dell — integrated so every room behaves the same way. Datacore also handled project management, system configuration, testing and user training for a seamless transition.

The result

A modern, tech-enabled workspace on a single AV standard, with distributed audio, wireless sharing and consistent control in every room — and headroom for future expansion.
