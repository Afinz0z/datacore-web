About Our PAVA Systems

A Public Address & Voice Evacuation (PAVA) system plays a critical role in protecting occupants during emergencies by delivering clear voice instructions and emergency notifications throughout a facility. Unlike conventional alarm systems, PAVA solutions provide spoken guidance that helps occupants respond quickly and safely during fire incidents or other emergencies.

Datacore's PAVA systems feature centralised control, multi-zone broadcasting, emergency prioritisation, and seamless integration with fire alarm and building management systems. Built for scalability, our solutions can expand to accommodate additional zones, buildings, or future infrastructure upgrades while maintaining reliable performance.
